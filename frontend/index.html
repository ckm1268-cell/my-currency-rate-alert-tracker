Real-time exchange rate monitoring app to track selling rates across online-published money changers in Klang Valley, Malaysia for 12 major currencies (USD, SGD, EUR, GBP, AUD, JPY, THB, KRW, CNY, HKD, VND, TWD). Evaluates live rates against user-defined target thresholds at user-selected intervals (5, 10, 15, or 30 mins) and sends alerts via Email, Telegram, or Push Notifications when target thresholds met. Ensure all comparison sections dynamically display all the selected monitored money changers per currency.
